Ghost Map

Where humanity left its footprints

Drag to turn the globe, scroll or pinch to zoom, and fly between the Moon and Mars to find the machines that stayed behind.

Moon · 3D view

Landing sites on the Moon

Surfaces are real NASA and USGS global maps. Body sizes keep their true proportions, but distances between worlds, lighting direction and rotation speed are compressed for viewing and are not to scale. Markers use rounded, published landing-site coordinates, so positions are approximate. Relief is exaggerated so craters and volcanoes are visible.

Surface map credits
  • Moon: CGI Moon Kit — LRO LROC WAC colour mosaic and LOLA elevation — NASA Scientific Visualization Studio. https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720
  • Mars: Viking Colorized Global Mosaic (MDIM 2.1) — NASA Ames / USGS Astrogeology Science Center. https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_viking_colorized_global_mosaic_232m
  • Mars: MGS MOLA global elevation model (surface relief) — NASA / JPL / GSFC · USGS Astrogeology Science Center. https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_mgs_mola_dem_463m
  • Earth: Blue Marble: Land Surface, Shallow Water, and Shaded Topography — NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / Visible Earth. https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/57752/blue-marble-land-surface-shallow-water-and-shaded-topography
  • Stars, Milky Way, nebulae, galaxies and the Sun are artistic illustrations, not real sky positions.
