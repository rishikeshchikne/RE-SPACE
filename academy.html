Learn

Lesson 1

What is space archaeology?

The study of human-made objects in space and on other worlds — spacecraft, landing sites, footprints and debris — as historical and cultural heritage.

Lesson 2

Why do spacecraft stay on the Moon and Mars?

Bringing hardware home needs fuel and mass that missions can't spare. Landers, rovers and lower stages are designed from the start to stay behind.

Lesson 3

Rover, lander or orbiter?

An orbiter circles a world from above. A lander touches down and stays in one place. A rover is a vehicle that can drive across the surface.

Lesson 4

How do scientists learn from old spacecraft?

By photographing sites from orbit, studying returned parts — like Surveyor 3's camera — and analysing archived mission data decades later.

Lesson 5

Why preserve mission history?

These sites record humanity's first steps beyond Earth. Protecting them keeps that story intact for scientists and future explorers.
