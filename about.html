About RE:SPACE

Every explorer deserves to be remembered.

RE:SPACE is an independent educational digital museum created for NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

Our mission

To tell the stories of NASA equipment left behind on the Moon and Mars, connecting engineering, exploration, science and human curiosity.

Why we created RE:SPACE

Most people know the launches and landings. Few know what happened to the machines afterwards. We wanted to give them their final chapter.

What you can discover

Artifact stories, a Ghost Map of landing sites, a mission timeline, and the Explorer Academy with lessons and a quiz.

Team

Built by a student team for NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, in the challenge "Abandoned but not Forgotten."

How we handle accuracy

  • Confirmed facts come from NASA mission pages and archives, linked on each artifact.
  • Approximate information — like map coordinates — is labelled as approximate.
  • Unknown information, such as Sojourner's final state, is stated as unknown.
  • Creative storytelling — the "If this machine could speak" sections — is clearly marked as imagined.
  • A mission ending means operations stopped; the hardware itself physically remains on the surface.
